## 🌟 Overview

Managing employee attendance and calculating monthly salaries manually is tedious, error-prone, and time-consuming. **AIET Employee Attendance & Salary System** solves this by offering a centralized dashboard that tracks day-to-day employee presence, manages leave requests, automates wage calculations, and generates payslips effortlessly. 

Whether you are an HR administrator or an employee, this platform provides clean role-based interfaces to handle daily operations smoothly.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍💼 For Admin / HR
* **Dashboard Analytics:** High-level overview of total employees, present/absent stats, and payroll summaries.
* **Employee Management:** Seamlessly add, update, or remove employee records and profiles.
* **Attendance Tracking:** Monitor daily clock-ins, log absences, and review historical logs.
* **Automated Payroll Engine:** Compute monthly earnings based on attendance, bonuses, and deductions instantly.

### 👩‍💻 For Employees
* **Self-Service Portal:** View personal details, employment status, and update credentials.
* **Attendance Log:** Keep track of individual punch-in/punch-out timings.
* **Salary History:** Access, view, and download monthly salary slips or earnings reports.
